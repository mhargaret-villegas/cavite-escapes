Aguinaldo Shrine

Category: Historical Landmark

The Aguinaldo Shrine is a significant historical landmark located in Kawit, Cavite. It is known for its connection to Philippine history and the country's independence.

The shrine is a meaningful destination for visitors who want to learn more about Philippine history while exploring Cavite.
