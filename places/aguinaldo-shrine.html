Aguinaldo Shrine

The Aguinaldo Shrine is one of the most important historical landmarks in Cavite. Located in Kawit, it is known as the place where Philippine independence was proclaimed on June 12, 1898.

I recommend this place not only for history lovers but also for anyone who wants to learn more about the Philippines and experience an important part of the country's story. The shrine allows visitors to explore its historical surroundings and appreciate the significance of the events connected to it.
